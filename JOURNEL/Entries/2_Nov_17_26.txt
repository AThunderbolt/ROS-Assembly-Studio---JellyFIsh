✅ Better Understanding of CMake

Learned what these actually do:

CMake
add_executable(JellyFish src/main.cpp)

Creates the executable target.

CMake
target_include_directories(JellyFish PRIVATE include)

Adds header search paths.

Equivalent idea:

Shell
-Iinclude
CMake
target_link_libraries(JellyFish PRIVATE glfw glm::glm)

Links external libraries.

Equivalent idea:

Shell
-lglfw
