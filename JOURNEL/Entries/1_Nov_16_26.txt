Project: ROS2 Assembly Studio (JellyFish)
Vision Clarification

The project goal became much clearer today.

Originally the project was loosely centered around graphics and a jellyfish concept, but the real product vision emerged:

Create a lightweight 3D assembly environment where users can visually build ROS2 robots and environments, then export directly to .xacro files without manually writing XML.

The long-term workflow:
